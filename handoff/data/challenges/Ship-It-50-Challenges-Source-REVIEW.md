## Classic Ship Landmarks — 100 points each

1. **The Piazza Grandeur** — A full team selfie on the sweeping central atrium staircase.
2. **Movies Under the Stars** — A photo with the massive poolside outdoor movie screen perfectly framed in the background.
3. **Under The Dome** — A high-angle group photo beneath the dramatic glass-domed top deck area.
4. **Miniature Masterpiece** — Locate the detailed scale model of a Princess ship in its glass showcase.
5. **Lotus Spa Zen** — A photo outside the spa doors with everyone striking their best over-the-top relaxed yoga pose.
6. **Front Row Center** — Sit in the absolute front row of the completely empty Princess Theater.
7. **Casino Neon** — A team photo outside the casino doors featuring a bright or flashing sign.
8. **Art Critic** — Posing next to your absolute favorite (or most confusing) piece in the onboard art gallery.
9. **Gym Buffs** — A snapshot of a teammate looking comically exhausted while "running" on a treadmill.
10. **Guest Services** — A photo with the main guest services desk clearly visible in the background.
## Food & Drink Discoveries — 200 points each

11. **International Café** — A photo highlighting a beautiful pastry display or a custom espresso drink cup.
12. **O'Malley's Irish Pub** — Capture the distinct green Irish pub decor or a classic pub menu board.
13. **Poolside Slice** — A close-up photo of a fresh, steaming slice of pizza from the deck counter.
14. **Soft Serve Swirl** — A photo holding a perfectly balanced complimentary soft-serve ice cream cone near the pool.
15. **Buffet Multi-Tasker** — A snapshot of a single plate featuring at least 5 completely mismatched types of food.
16. **Tropical Paradise** — Hold up a bright, colorful drink (cocktail or mocktail) complete with a fruit garnish or tiny umbrella.
17. **Main Dining Elegance** — Pose like royalty at an empty, fully-set dinner table before evening service begins.
18. **Sweet Treat** — Find the most colorful, multi-layered cake slice at the buffet dessert station.
19. **Good Spirits Mixology** — Capture a photo of a unique drink menu or the bartender's custom cocktail station.
20. **Room Service Delivery** — A photo of the classic hanging door menu or a fresh morning breakfast tray in your room.
## Quirky Details & Hidden Artifacts — 300 points each

21. **Carpet Compass** — A photo pointing to the hallway carpet pattern that indicates which way is the front of the ship.
22. **Stateroom Door Flair** — Find and photograph the most uniquely or creatively decorated stateroom door in the halls.
23. **Elevator Map** — A photo of the digital deck indicator screen changing numbers inside an elevator.
24. **Nautical Rose** — Locate a built-in compass rose design element embedded in the ship's flooring or woodwork.
25. **Safety First** — Stand side-by-side with an official, bright red "Star Princess" life ring buoy.
26. **Deck 13 Mystery** — A photo of the elevator button panel or stairwell sign confirming whether Deck 13 exists on the ship.
27. **High Wind Warning** — Posing with a unique nautical safety sign (like a "Watch Your Step" or "High Wind Area" door notice).
28. **Towel Zoo** — A photo of a creative towel animal greeting you on your stateroom bed.
29. **Lounge Spotlight** — Stand on an empty lounge stage while holding an imaginary microphone.
30. **Ocean Wake** — A snapshot from the absolute back of the ship looking directly down at the white churning water trail.
