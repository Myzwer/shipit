## At a glance

- **101** unique named cocktail candidates across the 13 game bars after excluding the last nine Good Spirits entries.
- **12 suggested cuts**, leaving **89** drinks in the random Drink Bounty pool.
- **77 possible rotation slots**: noon, 1 PM, …, 10 PM across seven days; each 10 PM draw remains available until 11 PM.
- **12 random cocktails would remain unseen** if every rotation uses a new draw and all slots run as planned.
- **Selection:** draw uniformly from eligible unused named cocktails; permanently remove the selected cocktail for this sailing. No tier quotas or guarantees.
- **Score:** Familiar 25 / Interesting 50 / Unusual 75 / Adventurous 100. Editorial oddity measures unexpected flavors, combinations, and preparations—not strength, number of shots, or how many bars stock it.

- **Price caveat:** the ten Good Spirits drinks shown here are **provisional / unpriced**; the supplied menu does not verify that they are covered by Princess Plus. We included them because the game organizer explicitly requested them.
- **Venue caveat:** Promenade, Lido, The MIX, and Star share a provisional pool-bar menu; these are four game locations but not four independently verified menus.

## Source discrepancies / checks before importing

1. **Passion Lilly is materially different across its two bars.** Bellini’s lists Montenegro Amaro, Italicus, lemon, passion fruit, vanilla, Prosecco; Cascade lists chamomile-infused vanilla vodka, lychee liqueur, passion fruit, pineapple, vanilla, Prosecco. A named-cocktail bounty may still accept either, but ingredient matching should be tied to the specific bar recipe.
2. **Frosé** at Princess Live includes grenadine, while the shared pool-bar version omits it. For ingredient bounties, do not automatically credit grenadine at pool locations.
3. **Sweetest Sounds**, **Spank Mint**, **Passion Satisfaction**, **Honey Sweet Bourbon**, **Frozen Aperol Spritz**, and **Aperol Frosé** have minor wording or ingredient-detail differences by venue. Keep source-specific recipes for matching.
4. **Good Spirits at Sea** remains unpriced. The first ten drinks were added by organizer choice; do not claim they are confirmed at $15 or below.
5. **Pool venue mapping** repeats the same shared PDF and is particularly uncertain for Star Bar. One listed bar does not prove the bar will serve that cocktail during the sailing.
6. **Actual onboard drinks and service hours can differ.** An unavailable bounty can expire; do not infer hours from this file.
